## Cohort Program: Agentic AI Engineering — Zero to Pro

TheTechX's cohort-based live training program for developers who want to build production-grade AI agents.

### Instructor

**Teja Vardhan Reddy Chennuru** — founder of TheTechX, instructor and program lead for all cohorts.

### Overview

- **Program Name:** Agentic AI Engineering: Zero to Pro
- **Duration:** 10 weeks
- **Sessions:** 50 live sessions, 1 hour per day
- **Format:** Live-only (no recordings or replays — attendance is mandatory)
- **Cohort Size:** Maximum 10 engineers per cohort (deliberately small for quality)
- **Frequency:** Once every 3 months, with selective intake
- **Target:** Developers comfortable with programming and APIs, new to agentic AI

### Who Is It For?

The program is designed for:
- Developers with general programming experience (any language) and familiarity with APIs
- People who are new to agentic AI specifically
- Prior LLM/prompting exposure is assumed
- A **Week 0 primer** is available for anyone who needs a foundation before starting

It is NOT a beginner coding bootcamp. You are expected to already write code.

### Curriculum

The program is divided into **4 Acts**:

#### Act I: Foundations (Weeks 1–3)
- How agent loops and tool-calling mechanics work
- Memory systems and context engineering
- Planning, reasoning, and self-correction approaches

#### Act II: Building Real Systems (Weeks 4–5)
- Tool design, environments, and human-in-the-loop gates
- Multi-agent orchestration and coordination patterns

#### Act III: Production-Grade (Weeks 6–8)
- Week 6: Frameworks — comparative analysis of LangGraph, CrewAI, Claude Agent SDK, and MCP server architecture
- Evaluation, observability, and failure analysis
- Production engineering and problem-framing

#### Act IV: Mastery & Capstone (Weeks 9–10)
- Frontier topics and capstone proposals
- Capstone sprint and Demo Day — present your agent system to the cohort with a written retrospective

### Projects & Deliverables

- **9 shipped projects** across the 10 weeks (one per week approximately)
- **Capstone agent system** — a full production-grade AI agent built and presented at Demo Day
- **Written retrospective** submitted alongside the capstone

### Enrollment Process

1. Fill in a **2-minute online application form** (name, email, background, motivation)
2. TheTechX responds within **48 hours**
3. Intake is selective — cohort size is capped at 10
