## What is TheTechX?

TheTechX is an India-based tech organisation founded by **Teja Vardhan Reddy Chennuru**. It operates across three areas, in order of its primary identity:

1. **Media & Knowledge** (primary brand) — Verified tech & AI news, curated tools, and engineering playbooks. The philosophy is honest analysis over hype: "No clickbait. No reposts. No hype."
2. **Services** (secondary brand) — "We also build." AI automation and custom software development for startups and enterprises, available on direct request.
3. **Education** (tertiary brand) — Cohort-based live training programs for developers, focused on Agentic AI Engineering (see curriculum.html on the website).

TheTechX operates as a small, focused team based in Hyderabad, India.
